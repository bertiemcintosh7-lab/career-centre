Bring in your LinkedIn

Not a login. LinkedIn will not give an app your positions, education or skills however politely it asks — but it will give you all of it, and you can hand that to this page.

  1. On LinkedIn: Settings & Privacy → Data privacy → Get a copy of your data.
  2. Pick the larger archive if offered; it arrives by email, usually within minutes.
  3. Unzip it and choose Positions.csv, Education.csv, Skills.csv and Profile.csv below — all at once is fine.

The files never leave this page. They are read in your browser, compared with what you have already typed, and nothing is saved until you press Apply. No password is involved at any point, and there is nothing to revoke afterwards.

The convergence

The highest-paying firms in London are also the leading football M&A practices. Kirkland advises private equity funds on team stakes — Arctos, and the 2023 Liverpool minority investment. Latham acted for Manchester United on the INEOS stake sale. Linklaters has a cross-practice sports team, and it runs insight days. You do not have to choose between the money and the football.

Paths and earnings

Read the spread, not the top. Football club chief executives at the very top earn several million — Ferran Soriano at Manchester City around £3.4m — but that is three or four people in the country. The middle of football pays well below law. The reliable money in sport is on the deal side, not the club side.

Where the sports work actually is

The trap worth avoiding: the best-known sports law names — Onside Law, Northridge, Morgan Sports Law — are boutiques that do not run vacation schemes or training contracts. They hire qualified lawyers laterally. Chasing them now is wasted effort; they are a destination, not an entry point.

The firms that do both sport and graduate recruitment are Bird & Bird (Legal 500 tier 1 for Sport), Kirkland and Latham on the deals side, and Charles Russell Speechlys and Brabners below the City pay bracket.

NQ pay as at August 2026 and moves annually. Sport column reflects Legal 500 London rankings.
